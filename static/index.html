Our shoes

Every pair is pre-loved and may be one of a kind. We aim to describe condition and visible wear honestly. Prices are in Kenyan shillings (KSh).

Payment

STK Push payments are confirmed by the server using Safaricom callbacks or transaction verification. Never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone, including shop staff.

Delivery and pickup

Choose pickup or delivery at checkout. Any configured delivery fee should be displayed before you confirm payment. Orders are prepared after payment confirmation.

Returns and refunds

Contact the shop promptly if an item is incorrect or has damage that was not disclosed. Return eligibility and refund arrangements should be agreed with the shop in line with applicable consumer protection requirements.

Privacy

Your name, email, phone number and delivery details are used to process orders and provide support. Payment PINs should never be collected by this website. Keep access credentials private.
